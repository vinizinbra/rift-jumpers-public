A two-minute read before your first run. The build has everything unlocked, so here's what to look for.

02 How a run works

About 18 minutes. Survive and Break repeat four times, then the boss.

Survive ~3 min

The horde never stops. Kill, grab XP, and pick an upgrade every level-up.

Explore anytime

Leave the path. Chests, secrets and optional challenges hide in the map.

Break ~1 min

Clear the area, then spend Coins at the Store and the Gunsmith.

Boss finale

Grab every reward you can first. Then beat it to win the run.

Every map is procedural · every run plays out differently

03 What new players miss

The things that make a run strong, and that nobody tells you.

🗺️

Optional challenges = the best rewards

Team challenges and timed traversal puzzles are hidden around the map. They're optional, and they pay out rare rewards you won't get anywhere else.

🛡️

Your accessory saves you once

It blocks one hit, then pops off and lands nearby. Walk over it to put it back on. Lost durability can be repaired at the Store during the Break.

🔫

You don't have to swap guns

Love your weapon? Keep it. Skip the new guns in the Store and make yours stronger at the Gunsmith instead: one upgrade per Break.

🔄

The Store restocks every Break

New weapons, food and repairs every time, so check it on every Break, even if it had nothing you wanted last time.

⛰️

Use the height

The map isn't flat. Ledges, ramps and gaps give you escape routes and safer angles in a fight.

📍

Ping and stick together

Ping elites, stores and challenges for your team. Solo works, but co-op is where the chaos lives.

Break checklist

1. Clear the last enemies.
2. Store: food, accessory repair, maybe a new gun.
3. Gunsmith: one upgrade for the gun you keep.
4. Everyone ready? Hold F / L1 to skip ahead.
