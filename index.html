The game

Survive together.

Someone blew up the Rift Heart, and now its shards are mutating everything they touch. The whole galaxy wants them. Your crew’s plan is simple: find shards, shoot stuff, get paid.

Rift Jumpers is a top-down shooter that uses height and traversal. Most survivors games have no map, or a map that doesn’t matter. Here it does: every run is a new procedural world full of hidden chests, shrines and challenges, while the horde keeps coming.

Runs last about 18 minutes. Enemies scale with time and team size, breaks between waves let the crew shop, heal and explore, and each run builds to a boss fight in a sealed arena.

  • Solo or co-op for 1–4Online co-op with shared XP, revives and pings. The horde scales with your party.
  • Deep buildsAbout 35 weapon perks, ranked Ascensions, Rift Mutations.
  • Elemental reactionsMix Fire, Ice and Lightning to set off Thermal Shock, Overload and Shatter.
  • Breaks between wavesStore, Gunsmith, healing shrines and Cursed Rifts.
  • Bosses and challengesBoss arenas, traversal puzzles, optional team challenges.
  • Permanent progressEarn Rift Shards for talents, then push from Easy into Nightmare.
